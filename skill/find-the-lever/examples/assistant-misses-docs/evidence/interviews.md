# Interviews — workspace assistant, "can't find my doc"

*Synthetic example data.* Six 30-minute remote interviews, weeks 8–9. Participants recruited from users who left a thumbs-down on a "no answer" response.

**P1 · Account manager, sales**
- "I wrote the renewal notes this morning, asked it to summarise them before my call, and it said it couldn't find anything. So I just opened the doc myself."
- "I assumed it reads everything I can read."

**P2 · Support lead**
- "When it says nothing found, I don't know if it looked and failed or never looked. Either way I stop asking."
- "It used to be good at this. Something changed about a month ago."

**P3 · Engineering manager**
- "Most of my working notes are in my notes app. I didn't realise that isn't connected."
- "If it told me where it searched, I'd know to look elsewhere instead of blaming it."

**P4 · Marketing manager**
- "Older docs it finds fine. It's the stuff from today it misses — and that's what I need it for."
- "I tried rewording it three times."

**P5 · Operations analyst**
- "Sometimes it gives me the wrong version of a doc, an older one with a similar name."
- "I'd use it more if I trusted the answer was the latest."

**P6 · Finance partner**
- "Honestly I might have misremembered the title. But it should say 'I found nothing called that' instead of a generic shrug."

## Themes (researcher's notes)
- **T1 Assumed omniscience** — 2 of 6 said outright they assumed it searches every source they can access (P1, P3).
- **T2 Fresh docs miss** — 3 of 6 described failures on docs created the same day (P1, P2, P4).
- **T3 Silent failure erodes trust** — 4 of 6 said "nothing found" gave them no way to tell why, and they stopped asking (P2, P3, P5, P6).
- **T4 Unconnected sources** — 1 of 6 keeps key work in an unconnected notes app (P3).
- **T5 Wrong version** — 1 of 6 got an older doc with a similar name (P5).
